Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what we collect when you join the pilot, why, who handles it, and how you can ask us to change or delete it.

Who we are

Granvaya turns daily current affairs into syllabus-linked notes for UPSC aspirants and revises them with you on a schedule. We are currently running a small, free early-access pilot. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Granvaya. You can reach us at support@granvaya.in.

What we collect

The only place this website asks you for personal information is the pilot signup form. It asks for:

  • your name and email address;
  • your phone or WhatsApp number (optional);
  • your target exam year and where you are in your preparation;
  • the newspapers you read.

We do not ask for anything else, and you can leave the optional field empty. Like any website, the services that deliver this page to you (our host and Google, see below) may automatically log technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit.

Why we use it

  • to contact you about pilot seats, launch news and your signup;
  • to understand who the product is for, so we can decide which newspapers and topics to cover first;
  • to answer you when you write to us.

We do not use your details to make automated decisions about you.

Who handles your data

  • Google Forms. When you submit the signup form, your answers are received through Google Forms and stored in Google’s systems, where our team can read them. Google’s own privacy policy applies to that handling.
  • Vercel. This website is hosted on Vercel, which serves the pages and may keep standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts. The site’s typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google when you open a page.
  • Social links. If you follow a link to our Instagram or LinkedIn profile, that service’s own policy applies from that point.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers above, or if the law requires us to.

Cookies and tracking

Our own pages do not set cookies, and they do not use analytics or advertising trackers. The third-party services above may set or read their own cookies when your browser contacts them.

How long we keep it

We keep your signup details for as long as the pilot is running and we need them to contact you, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can email us at any time to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to withdraw your consent, or to stop hearing from us. Write to support@granvaya.in from the address you signed up with and we will act on it promptly.

Young people

Granvaya is meant for exam aspirants who are adults. If you are under 18, please do not submit the signup form without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers and limit who on our team can see signup details. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we take care of the information you give us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. This policy is written with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.
